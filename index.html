About

The Journey

I'm Anthony "Tony" Kim, from Orange County, SoCal. I graduated from UC Berkeley with an econ degree and a background in bio and finance. My senior thesis was on birth rates in the United States.

Shortly after graduating, I joined the Marcus & Millichap research team in Phoenix as a research associate. I built market and investment reports on metros across the US, covering all five asset classes, for our brokers and their clients.

I quickly realized I wanted more autonomy, more ownership and the energy of a startup. I moved to San Francisco and joined Cloud Storage Security as a BD analyst. A few months later I became its Marketing Lead, in charge of the entire marketing function.

I like having a seat close to decisions and close to outcomes. Seeing the direct results of my work in a fast paced environment gives more meaning to the effort I put in.

"Adventure is out there."
I take a adventure + curiosity driven approach to my life both personally and professionally. I like to learn, push the boundaries of what I know and can do, and discover the unknown. Outside work I enjoy recreationally producing music, solo camping, fishing, training Muay Thai and hitting the gym.
